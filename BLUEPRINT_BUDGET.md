## 💡 Engineering Design: The Modular Approach
The Nerve uses a **Modular Motherboard Architecture**. To prevent mechanical stress on the PCB and allow for enclosure flexibility, all primary interfaces (Joystick, Encoders, Switches, OLED) are mounted into the 3D-printed case and hand-wired to the PCB via onboard screw terminals (U1-U4) and JST headers (U5).
